About

I’m a machine learning engineer at Meta, focusing on the safety and alignment of large language models. My work spans fine-tuning, safety data curation, and large-scale evaluation to reduce harmful outputs and improve user experience.

Before Meta, I co-founded Variational AI, where we used deep generative models—such as VAEs and diffusion models—to design novel drug-like molecules and accelerate early-stage drug discovery.

Prior to industry, I completed my PhD in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University under the supervision of Prof. Greg Mori, and earned my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology.

Variational AI

Co-founder & Machine Learning Scientist (2019–2024)

At Variational AI, we built generative models to propose new molecular structures with desired pharmacological properties. By combining diffusion models, VAEs, and structure-based priors, we searched chemical space for promising drug candidates far more efficiently than manual design.

  • Led a small multidisciplinary team of ML researchers, software engineers, and computational chemists.
  • Developed generative pipelines for molecular design and property-conditional sampling.
  • Worked across modeling, data, and engineering to turn research ideas into production systems.
