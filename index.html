About

I am a machine learning engineer at Meta, where I work on the safety and alignment of large language models. My work focuses on improving the reliability of LLMs through better training signals, safety evaluations, and model behavior analysis.

Previously, I co-founded Variational AI, a generative AI drug discovery startup. At Variational AI, we built deep generative models to design novel molecular compounds with the potential to become new medicines, exploring chemical space more efficiently than traditional approaches.

I completed my PhD in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University under Prof. Greg Mori, working on machine learning and computer vision, and spent part of my PhD as a visiting researcher at the National Institute of Informatics in Tokyo. Before that, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology in Iran.
