I am curently a machine learning researcher at Quadrant AI (a D-Wave sys. business) in Burnaby, Canada. I am interested in developing simple and effective machine learning algorithms that are applicable in practical domains such as computer vision.

I am also a PhD in computer science at Simon Fraser University under supervision of Prof. Greg Mori. In my PhD my focus is on handling limited data in machine learning tasks. I was lucky to do my M.Sc. in computer science under supervision of Dr. Mori at SFU. Prior moving to Canada, I was living in Iran, where I recieved my B.Sc. degree from Sharif University of Technology. from Iran, where I finished my undergraduate studies at Sharif University of Technology..
