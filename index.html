I am curently a machine learning researcher at Quadrant AI (a D-Wave sys. business) in Burnaby, Canada. I am interested in developing simple and effective machine learning algorithms that are applicable in practical domains such as computer vision.

Before joining Quadrant AI, I did a number of amazing research internships during my PhD at tech companies such as Google Reserach (Seattle), Microsoft (Redmond), and D-Wave (Burnaby). I also lived for almost one year in Japan, where I was doing a reserach internship at National Institute of Informatics (Tokyo).

I am also, currently, a PhD student in computer science at Simon Fraser University under supervision of Prof. Greg Mori, expecting to graduate soon. In my PhD my focus is on handling limited data in machine learning tasks. I was lucky to recieve my M.Sc. under supervision of Prof. Greg Mori at SFU.

Before moving to Canada, I was living in Iran, where I recieved my B.Sc. degree from Sharif University of Technology.
